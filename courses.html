Courses & tuition · Art Junction

Unlock your creativity

Michael teaches at Art Junction — in the studio, online and one-to-one — for complete beginners and experienced artists alike. Topics include drawing with charcoal, fabricating steel and casting in bronze.

In Michael's words

Being a tutor is not about me or my work. It's about bringing the best out of other people — helping them to progress, helping them to achieve.

Michael's teaching looks beyond the representational, into the imaginative recesses of the creative mind. He encourages the brave steps and artistic risks that unlock creativity, and the confidence to take on fresh subjects: sounds, feelings, emotions or actions.

Equally at home drawing, painting or sculpting, he moves freely between 2D and 3D. Whatever your level, he will look at and discuss your work in a respectful, helpful and constructively critical way.
